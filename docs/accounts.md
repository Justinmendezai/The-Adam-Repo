# Accounts — the only signup you do

Coding agents can clone Adam, install skills, and wire a project. They **cannot** create your GitHub, Vercel, or Neon login. That first signup is the remaining friction.

When a video is recorded, drop the URL in the table. Until then, Adam should sit with you: why you need it, wait until the account exists, then **you (the agent) integrate**.

| Account | In one sentence | Video |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| [GitHub](https://github.com/signup) | Where the project is saved and shared | _not recorded yet_ |
| [Vercel](https://vercel.com/signup) | Puts the site on the internet | _not recorded yet_ |
| [Neon](https://console.neon.tech) | The database | _not recorded yet_ |

You do not need all three on minute one. GitHub is first (so the project has a home). Vercel and Neon when Adam is ready to put something online or store data.

## For the agent

Do not send a signup homework list at the start of calibrate.

When you actually need an account:

1. One sentence why, in their technical level.
2. Link the matching row above (and the video URL once it is filled in).
3. Wait until they say the account exists. Do not walk them through every form field if a video exists — that is the video’s job.
4. **You** do the integrate: plugin, CLI login, env vars, project link. Do not paste a command checklist for them to retype.
5. Never ask them to paste secrets into chat. Use the host’s secret/env flow.

If they do not need a public site or a database yet, do not mention Vercel or Neon.
